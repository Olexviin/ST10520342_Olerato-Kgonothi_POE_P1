Our Programs

Discover how we empower South African students through technology and education.

Laptop Distribution Program

Our flagship programme provides refurbished laptops to underprivileged high school students. Each laptop is loaded with essential educational software, including Microsoft Office, learning apps, and both online and offline resources.

Programme Highlights

  • Each student receives a fully refurbished laptop with a 12-month warranty.
  • Students receive training on how to use the device effectively for learning.
  • We partner with schools to identify students in greatest need.
  • Over 450 laptops distributed to date.
Join Our Programme

Internet Access Program

We partner with mobile network providers to offer subsidies or free internet data to students, ensuring they can access online learning resources, research materials, and submit assignments

Programme Highlights

  • Free Wi-Fi access at partnered schools/campuses.
  • Mobile data packages for students.
  • Access to online learning platforms and educational resources.
  • Over 200 students currently benefiting from this programme.

Digital Skills Training

We offer workshops and training sessions to help students develop essential digital skills, including computer literacy, coding basics, and digital research techniques.

Programme Highlights

  • Monthly workshops are held at partnered schools.
  • Curriculum covers basic computing, digital research, and coding fundamentals.
  • Students receive certificates of completion.
  • Over 600 students trained to date.
Sign Up for Training

Mentorship Programme

We connect students with professional mentors from the technology and education sectors, providing guidance, career advice, and inspiration for their future careers.

Programme Highlights

  • One-on-one mentoring sessions with industry professionals.
  • Career guidance and university application support.
  • Networking opportunities with corporate partners.
  • Over 600 students currently enrolled in the programme.

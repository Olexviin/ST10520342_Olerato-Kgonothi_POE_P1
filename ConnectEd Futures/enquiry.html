How You Can Help

Become A Donor/Sponsor

Your corporate sponsorship can provide laptops, internet access, and training to dozens of students. We work with you to create a customised sponsorship package that meets your CSR goals.

  • Bronze: R10,000 - Provides 10 laptops
  • Silver: R25,000 - Provides 25 laptops + training
  • Gold: R50,000 - Provides 50 laptops + training + internet access
  • Platinum: R100,000+ - Comprehensive school programme

Volunteer Your Skills

We need passionate volunteers to help us refurbish laptops, train students, and mentor young people.

  • IT Volunteers: Help refurbish and repair donated laptops.
  • Trainers: Assist with digital skills workshops at partner schools.
  • Mentors: Guide students in their academic and career journeys.
  • Administrative Support: Assist with operations and fundraising.

Donate Old Laptops

We accept gently used laptops, tablets, and other technology equipment. All devices are refurbished and distributed to students in need.

  • Laptops (working or needing repair)
  • Tablets and smartphones
  • Computer peripherals (keyboards, mice, monitors)
  • Networking equipment
